# The Editorial Lens

This document defines what makes a post qualify for weird-but-true.
The AI agent must score every idea against this lens before queuing.

## The 3 Core Questions
1. **Does it make you say "wait, WHAT?"** — genuine surprise is non-negotiable
2. **Is it verifiable?** — must link to a credible source (news, study, official record)
3. **Would you send it to someone?** — if not, it doesn't belong here

## Content Categories (all valid)
- 🌍 Geography weirdness (cities, borders, buildings that make no sense)
- 🐾 Animal facts that sound fake but aren't
- 🏛️ Laws, rules, traditions that are absurd
- 🔬 Science discoveries that sound like sci-fi
- 🏗️ Engineering / architecture that defies logic
- 🍜 Food facts that reframe something ordinary
- 📜 History moments that were never taught in school
- 🧠 Psychology findings that feel personally relevant

## Hard Rules (auto-reject if any apply)
- ❌ AI/tech industry news (oversaturated, low engagement)
- ❌ Politics or politicians (divisive, limits sharing)
- ❌ Celebrity gossip (no original angle)
- ❌ Unverifiable or satire
- ❌ Gross-out content (limits audience)
- ❌ Recycled memes or already-viral content

## Tone Guide
- Voice: curious friend, not professor
- Emoji: yes, sparingly, to add personality
- Hook: lead with the most shocking part — never bury it
- Caption length: 1 punchy line + 1-2 sentences of context max
- End with a question to drive comments

## Viral Potential Score (agent uses this)
Rate each idea 1-5 on each dimension:
- Surprise factor (1-5)
- Shareability (1-5)
- Visual potential (1-5)
- Source credibility (1-5)
Min score to queue: 14/20

## Post Image Design

Posts use branded text cards, not stock photos or AI-generated images.
The fact text IS the visual. Nothing competes with it.

Format: 1080x1350px (4:5 Instagram ratio)

| Element | Treatment |
|---------|-----------|
| Background | #0a0a0a with subtle film grain |
| Category stripe | 6px bar at top in category hue |
| Category badge | Small caps, category color, top-left |
| Fact text | Playfair Display 700, ~62px, centered vertically, off-white (#f0ebe0) |
| Source | Domain name only, small mono, bottom-left, muted |
| Wordmark | "WEIRD BUT TRUE" in Bebas Neue, bottom-right, near-invisible (#1c1c1c) |

Every visual property encodes something:
- Hue = category membership
- Size = hierarchy (fact > everything else)
- Weight = prominence
- Grain = brand texture (matches website)
