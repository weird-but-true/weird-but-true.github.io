### Key Finding
AI/tech content: high volume, low engagement (avg 2.3K likes)
Curiosity/humor content: low volume, massive engagement (avg 2.1M likes)
→ Opportunity gap confirmed: curiosity niche is underleveraged relative to performance

### Patterns That Drive Sharing
1. Geographic weirdness (chinainsider model) — works globally
2. Nostalgia + chaos (memezar model) — hard to replicate without archive
3. Science that sounds like fiction — strong saves + shares
4. Anything that ends with "...and it actually works / is real"

### What to Track Going Forward
- Saves rate (higher = algorithm boost)
- Share rate (most important for growth)
- Comment sentiment (questions = good, debates = great)
- Which categories hit >10K likes consistently
