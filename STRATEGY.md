# Strategy

## Origin
This page concept was derived from a live Instagram feed analysis session (May 2025).
Real engagement data was collected by scrolling and scraping post metrics.

## Data Summary (seed session)

| Theme | Avg Likes | Max Likes | Posts Sampled |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meme/Humor | 10.5M | 21M | 2 |
| Viral Curiosity | 2.1M | 21M | 10 |
| Celebrity/Pop | 581K | 581K | 1 |
| News/Politics | 15K | 40K | 3 |
| AI/Tech | 2.3K | 9K | 8 |
| Lifestyle/Wellness | 2.3K | 4.6K | 2 |

## Key Insight
AI/tech content is the **most common but least engaging** niche on the feed.
Meme/humor and viral curiosity content dominates by 1000x.

Top performers observed:
- `memezar` — 21M likes (nostalgia prank chaos)
- `9gag` — 20M likes (pure meme)
- `chinainsider` — 229K likes (weird China facts/videos)
- `morningbrew` — 13K likes (Sam Altman news framing)

## Why "Weird But True" Wins
- Curiosity gap is the single most powerful engagement mechanic
- Verifiable = more shareable ("omg is this real?!" comments drive reach)
- Not dependent on being funny — just finding good source material
- Underserved: memezar/9gag recycle old internet. We do *current, sourced, real*
- 1 post/day is sustainable with AI assistance

## Competitive Edge
- chinainsider proves regional weirdness goes viral globally
- We expand that globally — weird things from any country/field
- Tone: warm, amazed, never cynical

## Growth Hack (Day 1)
Publish first 9 posts on launch day to make profile look established.
Then maintain 1/day cadence.
